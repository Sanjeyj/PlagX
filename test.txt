The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Plagiarism detection is a technique of finding instances of plagiarism within a work or document. The use of specialized software or online services can help educators and content creators identify copied text. Natural language processing and vector search are modern approaches to semantic similarity detection. This system uses sentence-transformers to convert text blocks into dense vector representations. Then, FAISS is used to perform high-speed nearest-neighbor search. Finally, a hybrid scoring engine combines exact matching with semantic match scores to generate a detailed report.
